MN privacy policy

MN (Mission Next) · Last updated 5 November 2026

MN is a maths learning app for children aged 3 to 16. It is made to keep children’s data on the family’s own phone. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

In short: without signing in, everything stays on the phone. A grown-up can choose to sign in with Google to back up and sync progress. Then that progress is stored in the family’s own private space in the cloud, which only that grown-up’s account can open. Children never sign in.

Who we are

MN is made by Moulik, an independent developer in India. Questions about privacy: see “Contact” below.

What stays on the phone

MN saves these on the phone itself:

  • each child’s first name, age and picture choice (an animal avatar);
  • levels played, answers, stars, coins and rewards;
  • settings such as the daily time limit, quiet hours and the grown-up PIN (stored as a one-way code, not the PIN itself).

It stays on the phone unless a grown-up saves a report card file or signs in with Google. If MN is uninstalled, it is deleted.

Optional: signing in with Google (grown-ups only)

Signing in is never needed to learn or play. It sits behind the grown-up lock. If a grown-up signs in:

WhatWhy
The grown-up’s Google account email, name and account IDTo know whose family space to open
Each child’s progress, as listed aboveAutomatic backup, restore on a new phone, and the same progress on more than one phone

This is stored with Google Firebase, in Google’s Mumbai (asia-south1) data centre. Each family’s space can be read only by the grown-up who signed in. We don’t look at, share or sell it. We use it only to back up and sync.

To delete it, sign out and choose “Delete cloud copy” in the Parent area, or write to us (see Contact). We delete it within 30 days. Progress on the phone itself is not affected.

Report card files

A grown-up can save a report card (an .html file with the child’s progress inside) or a printable worksheet. These files are made only when the grown-up asks. They go wherever the grown-up saves or shares them, and MN doesn’t send them anywhere.

Notifications and widgets

MN can show reminders, such as a treat jar about to melt or a backup being due, and it has home-screen widgets. These are planned and shown on the phone itself. No server is involved.

Permissions MN asks for

  • Notifications, for the reminders above (you can say no).
  • Run at start-up, to put the reminders back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration, for gentle buzzes on answers.
  • Internet, only for Google sign-in and backup, and for reading voices that the phone’s speech engine fetches online.
  • Photos: only if a grown-up picks a photo of a reward. It stays on the phone.

Children’s privacy

MN is for children, so it is built to collect as little as possible. Children never make an account, type an email or see sign-in screens. The parent or guardian decides whether to sign in, and they can see, change or delete the child’s data at any time in the Parent area. This follows India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Google Play’s Families policy. We don’t knowingly collect anything else from children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking libraries.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No sharing with anyone, except the cloud storage provider named above, which stores it for us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with a new date at the top. If a change affects what is collected, the Parent area will say so first.

MN privacy policy

MN (Mission Next) · Last updated 7 November 2026

MN is a maths learning app for children aged 3 to 16. It is made to keep children’s data on the family’s own phone. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

In short: without signing in, everything stays on the phone. A parent can choose to sign in with Google to back up and sync progress. Then that progress is stored in the family’s own private space in the cloud, which only that parent’s account can open. Children never sign in.

Who we are

MN is made by Moulik, an independent developer in India. Questions about privacy: moulikmishra11@gmail.com.

What stays on the phone

MN saves these on the phone itself:

  • each child’s first name, age and avatar (a cartoon animal chosen in the app; MN never takes photos of children);
  • levels played, answers, stars, coins and rewards;
  • settings such as the daily time limit, bedtime and the Parent PIN (stored as a one-way code, not the PIN itself).

It stays on the phone unless a parent saves a progress file or signs in with Google. If MN is uninstalled, it is deleted.

Optional: signing in with Google (parents only)

Signing in is never needed to learn or play. It sits behind the parent check. If a parent signs in:

WhatWhy
The parent’s Google account email, name and account IDTo know whose family space to open
Each child’s progress, as listed aboveAutomatic backup, restore on a new phone, and the same progress on more than one phone
The phone’s MN code, a label like “Phone K7Q4-MZ2P”, the app version, and when MN was last openedSo the person who gave you MN can approve your account and see which phones use it

The version of MN shared by invitation (not the one on Google Play) asks for approval: when you sign in there, the person who gave you MN sees your email and name, so they can approve your account (or remove it later). The Google Play version needs no approval.

This is stored with Google Firebase, in Google’s Mumbai (asia-south1) data centre. Each family’s space can be read only by the parent who signed in. We don’t look at, share or sell it. We use it only to back up and sync.

Progress files and worksheets

A parent can save a progress file (an .html file with the child’s progress inside) or a printable worksheet. These files are made only when the parent asks. They go wherever the parent saves or shares them, and MN doesn’t send them anywhere.

Notifications and widgets

MN can show three kinds of reminder, each of which a parent can switch off: a daily learning-time note at a time the parent picks, a note when a reward is ready to hand over, and a note when a backup is due. None are sent at bedtime. MN also has home-screen widgets. All of this is planned and shown on the phone itself; no server is involved.

Focus plan and reports

The report and the weekly Focus plan (which picks the skills a child finds hardest) are worked out on the phone from the child’s answers. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them.

MN × purchases

MN × is bought through Google Play, behind the parent check. Google handles the payment; MN never sees card, UPI or bank details. Google Play tells the app only which MN × plan the Google account owns, so the app can switch the extras on. Google’s own privacy policy covers the payment.

Permissions MN asks for

  • Notifications, for the reminders above (you can say no).
  • Run at start-up, to put the reminders back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration, for gentle buzzes on answers.
  • Internet, only for Google sign-in and backup, and for reading voices that the phone’s speech engine fetches online.
  • Photos: only if a parent picks a photo of a reward. It stays on the phone.

Children’s privacy

MN is for children, so it is built to collect as little as possible. Children never make an account, type an email or see sign-in screens. The parent or guardian decides whether to sign in, and they can see, change or delete the child’s data at any time in the Parent area. This follows India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Google Play’s Families policy. We don’t knowingly collect anything else from children. If you think we have, tell us and we will delete it.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking libraries.
  • No selling or renting of data.
  • No sharing with anyone, except the cloud storage provider named above, which stores it for us.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Parent area → App → Delete account and data. This deletes everything MN keeps on the phone and, if you signed in, your family’s online backup and your MN sign-in, straight away.
  • Without the app: see how to delete your MN account and data, or email us from the Google account you signed in with. We delete the online backup and the sign-in within 30 days.
  • Uninstalling MN deletes everything on the phone. An online backup stays until you delete it as above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here, with a new date at the top. If a change affects what is collected, the Parent area will say so first.

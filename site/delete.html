Delete your MN account and data

MN, the maths app for children by Moulik. You can delete your family’s data from inside the app, or ask us to delete it if you no longer have the app.

In the app (straight away)

  1. Open MN and tap the ⚙️ button, then pass the parent check.
  2. Tap App on the right, then Delete account and data.
  3. Tap Delete everything.

This deletes every child’s progress, rewards and settings on the phone. If you signed in with Google, it also deletes your family’s online backup and your MN sign-in.

Without the app

Email moulikmishra11@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in to MN, with the subject “Delete my MN account”. We delete your family’s online backup and your MN sign-in within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: the children’s first names, ages, avatars and progress; settings; your Google email, name and account ID as MN holds them; the list of phones linked to your family.
  • Kept: nothing.
  • Not touched: your Google account itself, backup files you saved yourself, and an MN × subscription. Cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

See also the MN privacy policy.
